Everything your skin needs, nothing it doesn't

Six quiet tools that work together.

🌿

Daily Check-in

A one-minute ritual: how you feel, how your skin feels, any symptoms or triggers. It all becomes one skin score, and the app tells you whether that score was measured from a photo or estimated from what you logged. A selfie is never required.

📷

AI Skin Analysis

Add a selfie when you want a closer read: concerns mapped on your face, skin-quality factors, and probable causes drawn from your real logged habits, not guesses.

🌅

Morning & Evening Routine

A routine built by AI from your skin type, concerns and goals, and yours to edit step by step. Follow it one step at a time, build a streak, and see today's progress at a glance.

🥗

Wellness: Meals, Water & Sleep

Snap your plate and Dermaa breaks down the nutrition, then tells you what in that meal helps or works against your skin. Log water and sleep alongside it.

💬

Sage, your AI Skin Coach

A coach that actually knows your skin: Sage answers with your scans, routine and meals in mind — specific, kind, and honest. Your conversations are saved and searchable.

🗒️

Progress Journal

Every check-in on one calendar, your photos side by side, and a slider that puts week one against today — so change you can't feel day to day becomes something you can see.

Light and dark themes · adjustable text size and reduced motion · gentle reminders for your routine and check-in
