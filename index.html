Questions, answered

What is Dermaa?

Dermaa is a skincare tracking app for Android. You check in once a day, follow a morning and evening routine built for your skin, log meals, water and sleep, and ask Sage, the AI skin coach, anything about your skin. It brings it all together into one skin score and a journal that shows how your skin changes over time.

How does the AI skin analysis work?

Take a selfie and Dermaa's AI maps the concerns it sees on your face, rates skin-quality factors, and suggests probable causes from what you have actually logged — your routine, meals, sleep and triggers. A selfie is always optional: a check-in without one still counts.

Is Dermaa free?

Yes. Check-ins, your routine, the journal, stats and reminders are free and unlimited. A Premium subscription raises the daily limits on the AI features — see Free & Premium.

Is Dermaa a medical or dermatology app?

No. Dermaa gives general skincare and wellness guidance. It does not diagnose conditions and does not replace a dermatologist — for anything that worries you, see a qualified professional.

What happens to my photos and data?

They are used only to run the features you see, never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app. Read the privacy policy.
